Every noun is either a proper noun (معرفة) or a common noun (نكرة)

# Proper Noun (معرفة)

- Name/Natural proper noun
    - Hamza
    - Ahmad
    - ...
- Pronouns
    - He
    - She
    - ...
- ال
    - البيت

&nbsp;

# Common Noun (نكرة)

- Ends in Tanween, NO ال
    - No noun can be propper and common
    - Either has tanween and no ال, or has ال and no tanween
    - Proper nouns can end in tanween
    - Pronouns cannot have tanween
