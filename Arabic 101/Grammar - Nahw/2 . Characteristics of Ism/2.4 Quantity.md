# General Notes

There are two types of Plurals:

- Sound Plural ([2.3 Iraab (Status)](2.3%20Iraab%20%28Status%29.md))
- Broken Plural
    - Word gets plural by breaking the original singular noun

# Trick

Plural will be a sound plural if:

- The noun is an active noun:
    - Starts with a م
- Second letter is ا then the plural
- Ends with ة

&nbsp;

Everything else will be a broken plural
